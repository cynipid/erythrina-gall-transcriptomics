Main figure package
Original package: 17 October 2026
Final review update: 26 October 2026 (comments 25 and 26)

Final numbering: Figures 1-6. Figure_Index.tsv lists the PDFs, high-resolution
PNGs, plotting scripts, caption files and source tables. Every figure folder
contains its current individual PDF and 300-dpi PNG for manuscript use.

Figure 5 was reformatted as three vertically stacked panels on a 180 x 203 mm
canvas; its ten complete gene identifiers are 9 pt. Figure 6 remains 180 x 240 mm,
with its nineteen panel-c identifiers increased to 10 pt and more row spacing.
Values, candidate identities, ordering, colors, classes and axis ranges are
preserved. All seven original source TSVs are byte-identical to the baseline.
Figures 1-4 retain their original artwork and plotting scripts.

Main_Figures_Preview.pdf has nine pages. Figures 5 and 6 use full-size figure
pages followed by separate caption pages so their labels are not reduced to fit
long legends. All pages were visually checked. Imported artwork is explicitly
clipped and placed behind the heading/caption layer.

captions.json is the canonical caption source. The individual caption.txt files,
Main_Figure_Captions.txt and Main_Figure_Captions.docx include the previously
approved revisions. The Word file preserves species italics, italic statistical
P and log subscripts. No caption wording changed during the label-size repair.

To rebuild captions and the combined preview:
    python -m pip install -r requirements.txt
    python build_captions.py
    python build_preview.py
To rebuild individual figures in another output directory:
    python rebuild_all.py --outdir rebuilt_figures

Export_Audit.json records the current display and source-identity checks.
Reproduction_Audit.json preserves the original 17 October baseline audit;
its historical PNG comparisons predate the authorized Figure 5/6 display changes.
SHA256SUMS.txt identifies all distributed files. PDF creation metadata can differ
between runs. No alignment, expression fit, enrichment test, secretion prediction
or MEROPS search was rerun for this display revision.
