Fig. 5. Expression patterns within the annotation-based plant-defense candidate inventory. Membership was frozen before the final refined-mask expression overlay. (a) Final expression status of all 806 candidate Trinity gene clusters. (b) Numbers of GP-up and UP-up candidates within five overlapping groups from this inventory. Group-specific counts are not additive because some candidates belong to multiple groups. (c) Ten selected GP-up predicted classical-secretome candidates from the inventory. Candidates were SignalP-positive and lacked a predicted transmembrane region. Points are colored by candidate class, and labels show unshrunk log₂ fold changes for GP versus UP. The dashed line marks log₂ fold change = 1. The topology predictions indicate entry into the classical secretory pathway; extracellular localization and biological activity remain untested.
