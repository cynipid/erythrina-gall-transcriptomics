Fig. 4. Representative functional enrichment associated with differential expression in galled and ungalled E. variegata petioles. Currently resolved GO terms and significant KEGG pathways are shown for GP-up genes (a, b; blue) and UP-up genes (c, d; orange). Bars indicate the percentage of annotation-eligible Trinity gene clusters in each directional set assigned to a category, and labels show cluster counts. All displayed categories had BH-adjusted P < 0.05. Redundant GO terms were omitted from the figure but retained in Supplementary Table S11. The broad KEGG category metabolic pathways and the category chemical carcinogenesis-DNA adducts were omitted from panel b but retained in Supplementary Table S12. The latter shares all 14 GP-up contributing genes with metabolism of xenobiotics by cytochrome P450 and therefore does not provide an independent gene-level enrichment signal.
