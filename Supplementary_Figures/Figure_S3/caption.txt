Supplementary Figure S3. Volcano plot of differential expression between pupal and larval Q. erythrinae. Positive unshrunk log2 fold changes indicate higher transcript abundance in pupae and negative values indicate higher abundance in larvae. Pupa-up genes are shown in pink and larva-up genes in green. Differential expression required adjusted P < 0.01 and |log2 fold change| ≥ 1; dashed lines indicate these thresholds. Non-DE denotes genes with an evaluable adjusted P value that did not meet both criteria. The plot includes 13,915 genes with evaluable adjusted P values. Values of -log10 (adjusted P) above 50 are displayed at 50; this display cap does not alter the underlying adjusted P values or DEG classification.
