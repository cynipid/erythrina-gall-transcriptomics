Supplementary Figure S2. Pearson correlations among larval and pupal Q. erythrinae libraries. Correlations were calculated from the primary variance-stabilized expression matrix (blind = FALSE) containing 13,919 retained genes. Rows and columns denote three larval pools (L1–L3) and three pupal pools (P1–P3), each containing 10 individuals. Cell values show Pearson correlation coefficients; the color scale indicates correlation strength.
