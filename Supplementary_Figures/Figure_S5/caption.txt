Supplementary Figure S5. Principal component analysis of the final refined-mask E. variegata expression dataset. Each point represents one whole-petiole library, with three galled petioles (GP, blue triangles) and three ungalled petioles (UP, orange circles) collected from the same host tree during one sampling occasion (n = 3 petioles per condition). PCA was based on variance-stabilized counts. PC1 and PC2 explained 73.05% and 13.90% of the variance, respectively. GP and UP samples separated along PC1; GP samples showed a wider spread than UP samples, particularly along PC2.
