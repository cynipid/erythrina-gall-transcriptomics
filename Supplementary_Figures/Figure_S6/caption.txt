Supplementary Figure S6. Pearson correlations among E. variegata whole-petiole libraries. Correlations were calculated from the variance-stabilized final refined-mask expression matrix (blind = TRUE) containing 24,734 retained genes. Rows and columns denote three galled petioles (GP1–GP3) and three ungalled petioles (UP1–UP3) collected from one host tree during one sampling occasion. Cell values show Pearson correlation coefficients; the color scale indicates correlation strength.
