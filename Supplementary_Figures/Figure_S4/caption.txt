Supplementary Figure S4. MA plot of insect developmental-stage expression differences. Each point represents one of 13,919 retained genes. The x-axis shows log10(baseMean + 1), where baseMean is mean normalized count abundance, and the y-axis shows the unshrunk log2 fold change for pupa versus larva. Green and pink points indicate larva-up (n = 1,909) and pupa-up (n = 2,497) genes, respectively, under the primary criterion of BH-adjusted P < 0.01 and absolute log2 fold change >= 1. Non-DE genes (gray; n = 9,509) have an available adjusted P value but do not meet both criteria. Not tested genes (crosses; n = 4) have an unavailable adjusted P value and are shown at their actual finite fold-change estimates. The solid line marks zero and dashed lines mark log2 fold changes of -1 and 1. Legend counts are gene counts.
