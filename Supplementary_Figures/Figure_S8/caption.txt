Supplementary Figure S8. MA plot of gall-associated plant expression differences. Each point represents one of 24,734 retained genes after the 16,404-gene sequence-provenance mask and frozen count filter. The x-axis shows log10(baseMean + 1), where baseMean is mean normalized count abundance, and the y-axis shows the unshrunk log2 fold change for galled (GP) versus ungalled (UP) whole petioles. Blue and orange points indicate GP-up (n = 2,147) and UP-up (n = 1,383) genes, respectively, under the primary criterion of BH-adjusted P < 0.01 and absolute log2 fold change >= 1. Non-DE genes (gray; n = 21,032) have an available adjusted P value but do not meet both criteria. Not tested genes (crosses; n = 172) have an unavailable adjusted P value and are shown at their actual finite fold-change estimates. The solid line marks zero and dashed lines mark log2 fold changes of -1 and 1. Legend counts are gene counts.
